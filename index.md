I'm a doctoral researcher at University of Helsinki, where I study how the human mind processes language. My current work looks at previous language experience and new language learning: how does our native language help us in learning a completely new language – and can we lean on later-learned languages for support, too?

Outside the lab (and often, within it) I make illustrations, infographics and other visual support for education and science
communication.
